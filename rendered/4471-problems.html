The two problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so their dates come out with day and month swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. It reaches people through the app, email and the booking-v2 form. This is the bug #4471 describes. Fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. A few of these reports read as a date moved by one day (desk-notes.json); by tenant they are parser, and they are counted there.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People do what the hint says, and the date flips. It comes only through the form, only for the tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so no new reports come this way.

For the desk

  • A wrong-date report after 5 October is a new problem until its cause says otherwise. Do not merge it into #4471.
  • Merging on subject alone put two causes in one ticket. Check the cause before you merge.
